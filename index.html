Eleven cuisines, built around how you eat

Eating well rarely fails on willpower. It fails on logistics.

The supermarket, the restaurant menu, and the recipe on TikTok that doesn’t fit how you eat. Cherry handles all three.

One app for every food decision

From the swipe on the sofa to the last item in the basket. Scroll to see each part at work.

  1. 01

    Swipe your week into a grocery list

    Pick the cuisines you’re craving, set a budget, and swipe. Recipes share ingredients so nothing rots in the fridge, and everything merges into one list, grouped by supermarket section, with an estimated price on every line.

    Store out of something? Tap it and Cherry finds a substitute, then rewrites the recipes that used it.

  2. 02

    Save any recipe from TikTok or Instagram

    Tap Share, pick Cherry, keep scrolling. The recipe lands in your inbox adapted to your diet and allergies: the original ingredient on the left, your swap on the right, with the reason for each.

    Tap a swap to cycle alternatives. Any recipe page works too, just paste the link.

  3. 03

    Scan the menu, know what to order

    Photograph the menu, up to six pages. Cherry reads it against your profile and splits it in two: dishes you can order as they are, and dishes that need one small change.

    For those, you get the exact phrase to say to the waiter.

  4. 04

    Check any product in the aisle

    Point the camera at a barcode and get a straight answer: it fits your diet or it doesn’t, and exactly which ingredient is the problem.

  5. 05

    Shop together, live

    Share one list with your partner or flatmate. Every tick shows up on both phones the second it happens, and the list sits on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island while you shop.

    Your profile and your recipes stay private.
